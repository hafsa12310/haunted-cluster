# Product: The Haunted Cluster

The Haunted Cluster lets engineers hear the health of their system. It turns live
infrastructure metrics (latency, error rate, memory, request volume) into a
generative horror soundscape and a haunted-house visual, where each room is a service.

Target users are on-call engineers and developers who can't watch dashboards all day.
Sound lets them notice problems early, while an AI "ghost" narrator explains what is
going wrong in plain English and warns when a past incident is repeating.

This is a Kiroween 2026 hackathon entry in the Frankenstein category: it stitches
together infrastructure monitoring, generative audio, and an AI narrator.

## Product principles
- The spooky theme must always serve the function: every sound and visual maps to a real metric.
- Judges must be able to test it live: chaos can be triggered from the UI with no setup.
- Explanations from the ghost must be accurate and grounded in actual metric data.
